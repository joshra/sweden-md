# About

> **30-second overview:** The About section is not filler. It explains why Sweden.md exists, how its editorial standards work, and what readers can expect from its essays.

## Why it matters

Without a strong About section, a project like Sweden.md quickly fragments. Some articles drift toward tourism copy, some toward neutral encyclopedic summary, and some toward thin stubs with no interpretive frame. The About section exists to make the site's commitments visible.

## What lives here

This section holds the project origin and the editorial principles. Together they explain how Sweden.md wants to balance warmth, evidence, and context.

## Core tensions this category has to manage

The About section is where Sweden.md handles recurring editorial tensions early: readability versus depth, narrative voice versus factual rigor, and Taiwanese reader context versus Swedish institutional specificity. If these tensions are not made explicit, topic articles tend to drift toward either flat data dumps or unsupported personal commentary.

So this category is not just a style guide. It is where Sweden.md makes its tradeoffs visible before readers move into the main topic essays.

## How the child pages work together

These pages form a reading sequence rather than a loose list. `Origin` explains why the project exists. `Editorial Principles` defines quality thresholds.

Taken together, the About category shows not only what Sweden.md publishes, but how its public voice is kept coherent over time.
